# SwarmAI — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 October 2026_

SwarmAI is a personal assistant app operated by Priyam Bansal for a small group of invited users. This policy
explains what data SwarmAI handles and why.

## Data we process
- **Messages you send** to SwarmAI through the web app, WhatsApp or Telegram (text, voice notes, images), and your
  phone number or chat id so replies reach you.
- **Google data you choose to connect** — Gmail, Google Calendar and a SwarmAI spreadsheet in your Google Drive — used
  only to carry out requests you make (e.g. summarise emails, create events, log expenses).
- **Things you ask SwarmAI to remember**: notes, tasks, reminders, expenses, contacts and travel plans.

## How we use it
Only to provide the assistant's replies and the actions you approve. Actions that send, create or delete anything
(emails, calendar events, messages) require your explicit confirmation. We do not sell data, show ads, or use your data
for marketing.

## Third parties
- **OpenAI** processes message content to generate replies, transcribe voice notes and read images.
- **Meta (WhatsApp)** and **Telegram** deliver messages between you and SwarmAI.
- **Google** APIs are used for Gmail, Calendar and Sheets when you connect your account.

SwarmAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including
the Limited Use requirements.

## Storage and security
Data is stored in a private database controlled by the operator. Google access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access is
limited to the operator.

## Your choices
- Disconnect Google at any time in **Settings** or at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
- Unlink WhatsApp or Telegram in **Settings**, or stop messaging the number.
- Ask us to delete your account and all associated data by emailing bansalpriyam17@gmail.com; we delete it within 30 days.
